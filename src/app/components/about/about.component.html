The founder's journey

Amara Elahi grew up watching her grandmother twist silk and gold filaments on a wooden loom in Lahore. What began as childhood curiosity became a lifelong pursuit: to carry that rhythm of hand and heart into jewelry worn every day.

After years studying textile conservation in Europe, she returned home to open a small atelier—naming it The Twisted Threads for the signature technique that defines every piece we make.

Our mission

To create handmade jewelry that honors artisan skill, celebrates natural materials, and connects wearers to the makers behind each piece.

Our vision

A world where luxury means transparency, sustainability, and craft—a wardrobe of heirlooms built slowly and worn proudly.

Why handmade jewelry

Machine-made pieces can mimic appearance, but never the soul. Handmade jewelry carries subtle variations—the slight asymmetry of a hand-hammered surface, the warmth of metal shaped over flame. These imperfections are signatures, not flaws.

Craftsmanship philosophy

“We do not rush the twist. Every coil is set with intention, every stone seated with respect. Jewelry should outlive trends—it should gather stories.”

— Amara Elahi, Founder
