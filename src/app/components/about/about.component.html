Our Heritage

The Legacy of Weaving Twisted Fibers

For generations, our atelier has blended traditional craftsmanship with organic luxury. We design premium home textiles and everyday apparel built to endure.

Purity of Fiber, Integrity of Design

At The Twisted Threads, we believe that luxury is not just seen; it is felt. Every product in our atelier begins its journey as raw, carefully selected natural fibers. From premium organic merino wool to organic flax linen, our commitment to natural sourcing guarantees a pure skin feel and durable resilience.

Our signature double-weave technique, where twisted threads of contrasting colors are interlaced together, creates deep, dimensional patterns that celebrate the organic imperfections of traditional craftsmanship.
