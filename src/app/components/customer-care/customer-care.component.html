Caring for Woven Threads

The Twisted Threads uses premium silk and nylon blends designed for durability, but they still require gentle care to maintain their luster.

  • Keep it Dry: Avoid exposing your woven jewelry to water, lotions, or perfumes. Moisture can weaken the threads over time.
  • Gentle Cleaning: If the threads gather dust, lightly brush them with a soft, dry makeup brush. Do not use chemical jewelry cleaners.
  • Storage: Store your pieces flat in the provided velvet pouch or a jewelry box to prevent the threads from snagging or tangling with other items.

Caring for Natural Pearls

Pearls are organic gems and are delightfully delicate. Remember the golden rule of pearls: "Last thing on, first thing off."

  • Put your pearls on only after you have applied your perfume, hairspray, and cosmetics.
  • Wipe them gently with a soft microfiber cloth after each wear to remove any natural body oils.
  • Never store pearls in airtight plastic bags, as they need ambient moisture to retain their beautiful sheen.
